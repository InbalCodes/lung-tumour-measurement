# Reference measurements and the quality bar

Generated by `eval/build_reference.py` and `eval/reference_report.py` from the expert outlines in
RIDER-LungCT-Seg. Numbers below are from the run of 17 Nov 2026. Research use only.

## What the reference is

- 31 patients, 59 scans. 28 patients have a same-day pair of scans (test and retest).
- Every scan has one outlined primary tumour: a manual outline by a radiation oncologist, and an
  outline from the dataset authors' own automatic method.
- All scans are 1.25 mm slices, lung kernel, pixel size 0.51 to 0.91 mm.
- Tumour long axis ranges from 12.9 to 95.3 mm (median 37.6 mm). None is 10 mm or smaller, 18 of
  59 are between 10 and 30 mm, and 41 are larger than 30 mm. These are mostly masses, not small
  nodules.

## How it is measured

The manual RTSTRUCT contours are filled into voxel masks (a pixel counts when its centre is inside
the contour). The same code then measures any mask, expert or tool:

- Volume: voxel count times voxel volume.
- Long axis: largest edge-to-edge distance within one axial slice; the slice where it is largest.
- Short axis: largest extent perpendicular to the long axis, on the same slice.
- Mean diameter: average of the two.

Checks: our masks match the DICOM SEG masks published with the dataset (Dice 0.999 or higher on
the 8 files checked), and overlays on the CT slices were inspected by eye.

## The quality bar: same-day repeat noise of the expert

The two scans of a pair were taken minutes apart, so the tumour did not change. Any difference
between the expert's two measurements is measurement noise.

| Measurement (28 pairs) | Median absolute difference | 95% limits of agreement | Largest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Volume | 6.8% | -25% to +28% | 37% |
| Long axis | 2.2 mm (4.9%) | -9.1 to +7.9 mm | 13.0 mm |
| Short axis | 1.5 mm | -7.8 to +6.3 mm | 12.9 mm |

Smaller tumours are noisier: median absolute volume difference is 13.5% for the 15 pairs under
9,000 mm3 and 5.2% for the 13 pairs above it.

What this means for the tool: growth tracking stays at least as reliable as today if the tool's
own same-day repeat noise is no larger than these figures.

## Development and test split

`eval/reference/splits.json`: 10 patients for development, 21 for testing, split by patient and
balanced by tumour volume. Parameters are tuned on the development set only.

## Limits

- One expert drew the reference. Their own repeat noise is large, so agreement with this reference
  has a ceiling.
- 28 pairs is a small sample; the limits of agreement are rough.
- The set has no small or ground-glass nodules and a single slice thickness, so it cannot answer
  questions about those subgroups.
